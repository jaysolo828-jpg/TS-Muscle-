Privacy Policy

T&S Muscle · Therapy & Sneakers

Last updated: April 1, 2025

Overview

T&S Muscle is a fitness training app built by Therapy & Sneakers. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

By using T&S Muscle you agree to this policy. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13. If you are under 13, do not use this app.

What We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used to create and access your account

Profile Information

  • Name, age, sex, bodyweight, and experience level (provided during setup)

Training Data

  • Workout history, training goals, split type, and scheduled training days
  • Sets, reps, weights, and progress over time
  • Session check-ins and feature preferences

Social Data (only if Social Features are enabled)

  • Data shared with or visible to other users within the app

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver personalized training programs and progressive overload recommendations
  • To power AI coaching features — your workout context is sent to the Claude API (Anthropic) to generate coaching cues and insights
  • To enable social features when you opt in
  • To send training reminders via push notifications (only if you enable them)
  • To allow you to back up and restore your own data
  • To improve the app and fix issues

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

Third Parties

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Supabase Account authentication, data storage, social features supabase.com/privacy
Anthropic (Claude API) AI coaching cues and training insights anthropic.com/privacy
OneSignal Push notifications for training reminders onesignal.com/privacy

When workout data is sent to the Claude API, Anthropic processes it under their API data policy. By default, API data is not used to train Anthropic's models.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's servers with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Some preferences are also stored locally on your device.

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. Use a strong password and keep your credentials private.

Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time within the app
  • Export your data using the "Back Up My Data" feature in Settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Disable social features at any time in Settings
  • Turn off push notifications at any time in Settings or your device settings

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your data from our systems within 30 days.

Children

T&S Muscle is not directed at children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will delete that account and its data promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
